# Three supplementary glyphs

`unifont-upper-three.otf` retains only U+2144D, U+23236 and U+255FD from
GNU Unifont Upper 18.0.01. They are the three traditional counterparts absent
from the installed Noto Sans CJK collection. Distributed under SIL OFL 1.1
(the upstream font is dual licensed); see OFL.txt. All other added Chinese
outlines use the mainland Noto Sans CJK SC face. These three exceptional glyphs
have Unifont's pixel-shaped outline style.

Source: https://unifoundry.com/pub/unifont/unifont-18.0.01/font-builds/unifont_upper-18.0.01.otf
